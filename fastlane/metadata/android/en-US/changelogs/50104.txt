VRKA 5.1.4 hardens security, updates, and custom yt-dlp safety:

- Requires SHA-256 confirmation for untrusted custom yt-dlp forks and re-verifies binaries before execution.
- Blocks unsafe custom command flags and credential headers; disables backups and redacts failure URLs.
- Bounds updater HTTPS redirects (5 hops, GitHub hosts only) and verifies target versionCode before install.
- Enforces locked Gradle verification offline and simplifies user, security, and release docs.
