VRKA 5.1.4 hardens security, updates, and custom yt-dlp safety:

- Requires SHA-256 confirmation for custom yt-dlp forks and re-verifies binaries before running.
- Blocks unsafe custom command flags and credential headers; disables backups and redacts failure logs.
- Caps updater HTTPS redirects at 5 GitHub hops and verifies target versionCode before install.
- Locks offline Gradle verification and simplifies project docs.
