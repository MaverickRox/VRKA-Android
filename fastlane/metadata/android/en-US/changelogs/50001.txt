VRKA Android 5.0.1 repairs in-app application update detection for canonical stable releases.

- Recognizes the exact three-part release tag and matching APK filename.
- Rejects malformed or mismatched release metadata instead of reporting an invalid release as up to date.
- Validates HTTPS redirects, package/version metadata, downgrade protection, and the production signing certificate before installation.
- Includes regression coverage for release parsing, download validation, and installer checks.
